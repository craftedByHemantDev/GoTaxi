# GoTaxi Vision Document

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# 1. Project Overview

GoTaxi is a next-generation Indian mobility platform designed to provide safe, reliable, affordable, and technology-driven transportation services for individuals, families, businesses, and delivery partners.

Unlike traditional ride-hailing applications, GoTaxi aims to become a complete mobility ecosystem where users can book multiple transportation services from a single application.

The platform is designed with scalability, security, and user experience as its highest priorities.

---

# 2. Vision

To become India's most trusted, safest, and smartest mobility platform by delivering affordable transportation solutions through modern technology.

---

# 3. Mission

Our mission is to simplify transportation for everyone by offering secure, transparent, and intelligent ride booking services while creating sustainable earning opportunities for drivers.

---

# 4. Goals

The primary goals of GoTaxi are:

- Build a reliable ride-booking platform.
- Provide the safest travel experience.
- Create better earning opportunities for drivers.
- Deliver transparent fare calculation.
- Build customer trust through technology.
- Support future expansion across India.
