Document ID: GT-CUST-001

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

# 1. Purpose

The Customer Module provides all features required for a customer to register, book rides, make payments, manage trips, communicate with drivers, and access GoTaxi services through a single mobile application.

This module is the primary user-facing component of the GoTaxi ecosystem.

# 21. Business Rules

Customer must verify phone number.

Customer must verify email.

Customer profile must be completed before first ride.

Customer cannot book rides if account is suspended.

Customer cannot abuse referral system.

Wallet usage is optional.

Ride cancellation policy shall be controlled by Admin.
